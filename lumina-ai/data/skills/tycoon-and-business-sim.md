# Tycoon & Business Sim

Tycoon games (RollerCoaster Tycoon, Transport Tycoon, Game Dev Tycoon, Two Point Hospital) put the player in charge of **building and running a profitable business** — a park, transport network, hospital, studio. The core fantasy is entrepreneurship: turn an idea into a thriving, optimized money-making operation. Where city-builders emphasize space, tycoons emphasize the **economic engine** and its optimization.

## The Economic Core

At the heart is a **profit model**: **income − costs = profit**, which you must grow:
- **Income** — from customers/services (ticket sales, fares, products, patients treated). Drive it up via more customers, higher satisfaction, better pricing.
- **Costs** — construction, staff wages, maintenance, upkeep, loans. Keep them in check.
- **Profit → reinvestment** — earnings fund expansion, which grows income, in a self-reinforcing loop. Managing this financial engine *is* the game.

Every decision is ultimately economic: does this improve the bottom line, now or later?

## Customer Satisfaction & Pricing

Success runs through **happy customers**:
- **Satisfaction systems** — customers/patients/visitors have needs and happiness (wait times, quality, amenities, cleanliness). Satisfied customers spend more, return, and spread word; unhappy ones leave and cost you.
- **Pricing** — a core lever with real tension: charge more (higher margin, but fewer/unhappier customers) vs less (volume). Finding the profit-maximizing price is a classic tycoon decision (see pricing-strategy).
- Balancing **quality vs cost** and **price vs demand** is the economic puzzle.

## Growth & Expansion Loops

Tycoons are engines of **escalating scale**:
- **Expansion** — reinvest profits into more/bigger/better (new rides, routes, wards, products), growing capacity and income.
- **Unlocks & tech** — research/progression unlocks new options, tiers, and efficiencies, pacing the growth and keeping it fresh (see idle-and-incremental pacing).
- **Scaling complexity** — as the business grows, more systems interact (logistics, staff, competition), and new problems emerge at size. The challenge scales with success.

## Optimization Depth

The genre's core pleasure is **optimization** — tuning a complex system for maximum efficiency/profit:
- **Efficiency puzzles** — layout, logistics, staffing, supply chains, scheduling. Min-maxing the operation is deeply satisfying to the optimizer mindset.
- **Systems interaction** — production chains, customer flow, resource management (see city-builder production loops). Small tweaks with big profit implications reward experimentation.
- **Emergent economics** — supply/demand, competition, market dynamics create depth and problems to solve. There's always a next inefficiency to fix.

The "aha" of finding a more profitable configuration is the addictive core.

## Legibility & Feedback

With a complex economic system, **communicating financial and operational state** is essential (see data-visualization-design, city-builder legibility):
- **Financial reports, graphs, and overlays** (profit/loss, customer flow, satisfaction, bottlenecks) so players can *diagnose* what's helping or hurting the bottom line.
- Clear feedback on *why* profit rose/fell is what makes the optimization loop solvable and rewarding. Opaque economics frustrate.

## Structure & Goals

- **Scenario/campaign** goals (reach a profit target, build a specific enterprise) provide direction and challenge.
- **Sandbox/free-play** for open-ended empire-building, serving the pure optimizer/creative.
- **Failure states** (bankruptcy) create stakes, but should be recoverable/instructive, not arbitrarily punishing.

Design tycoon/business sims around a **clear economic core (income−costs=profit)** driven by **customer satisfaction and pricing decisions**, **growth-and-expansion loops** funded by reinvested profit, deep **optimization** of interacting operational systems, and strong **financial legibility** — delivering the satisfying fantasy of building and fine-tuning a thriving business into an ever-more-profitable, well-oiled money machine.
