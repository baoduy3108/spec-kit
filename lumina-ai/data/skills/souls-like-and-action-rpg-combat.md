# Souls-like & Action-RPG Combat

Souls-likes (Dark Souls and its lineage) reinvented action-RPG combat around **deliberation, commitment, and mastery-through-failure**. Every action matters, enemies are lethal, and difficulty is the point — not to frustrate, but to make victory *earned* and meaningful. The design philosophy: respect the player by challenging them.

## Deliberate, Stamina-Based Combat

Unlike button-mashing action games, souls combat is **slow, weighty, and committal**:
- **Stamina** gates everything — attacking, dodging, blocking, sprinting all drain it. You *can't* spam; you must **manage** stamina, choosing when to act and when to recover. This forces deliberate, thoughtful play.
- **Commitment** — attacks and dodges have startup and **recovery** frames you're locked into (see combat-systems-and-hitboxes). Swinging a big weapon commits you; whiffing leaves you vulnerable. Every action is a *decision with risk*.
- **Weighty feel** — animations have heft; there's no free cancel-everything. Combat is a tense negotiation of openings, not a flurry.

This deliberateness is the genre's signature — combat as a careful duel, not a spectacle.

## Readable Enemies & Telegraphs

Fair difficulty depends on **legibility**:
- **Telegraphed attacks** — enemies wind up with clear, learnable animations (**tells**) that signal *what* is coming and *when*. Skilled players read tells and respond.
- **Attack patterns** — enemies/bosses have consistent movesets the player *learns* over attempts. The challenge is pattern recognition + execution, not randomness.
- Bosses are elaborate, multi-phase pattern puzzles — the genre's centerpiece.

The player should always feel a death was *their* misread, not the game cheating. Deaths teach.

## Defense: Dodge, Parry, Block

The core interaction is **timing a defensive option against a read attack**:
- **Dodge/roll** — i-frames (invincibility) let you phase through an attack if timed right (see inverse... no — timing/i-frames). Spacing and timing the roll is the fundamental skill.
- **Block/shield** — safer but drains stamina; guard-breaking punishes over-blocking.
- **Parry** — high-risk, high-reward precise counter that opens a devastating riposte.

Each defense is a risk-reward choice against the incoming attack — the heart of moment-to-moment play.

## Risk-Reward & Learning Through Failure

- **High stakes** — death has consequences (souls/currency loss with a chance to recover, respawning enemies). This makes every step tense and every victory sweet.
- **Failure is the teacher** — you *will* die repeatedly; each death reveals a pattern, a tell, a mistake. Progress is the player getting *better*, not the game getting easier. The "fog of learning" clears through attempts.
- **The payoff** — overcoming a wall you failed at 20 times produces intense satisfaction. Difficulty *is* the reward mechanism.

## The Checkpoint Loop (Bonfires)

**Bonfires/checkpoints** define the rhythm: rest to heal and refill resources, but **respawn all normal enemies**. This creates the run-back loop — each attempt at a boss is preceded by traversing (and re-clearing) the path, which itself teaches and adds stakes. Checkpoint *placement* is crucial pacing (too far = tedious run-backs = the genre's main frustration; too close = no tension). Modern souls-likes often soften run-backs (fog gates near bosses).

## Difficulty as Design Philosophy

- **Hard but fair** — never cheap, always readable and learnable. Frustration should come from *your* mistakes, resolvable by improvement.
- **No hand-holding** — cryptic worlds, minimal tutorials; discovery and figuring things out is part of the respect.
- **Accessibility debate** — the intended difficulty is core to the experience; how (or whether) to add easier modes is a live design/values discussion.

Design souls-like combat around **stamina-gated, committal actions**, **enemies with readable telegraphs and learnable patterns**, **precisely-timed dodge/parry/block risk-reward**, and a **checkpoint loop with real stakes** — so that steep, *fair* difficulty turns every hard-won victory into earned mastery. The genre's core belief: challenge, done respectfully, is the deepest reward.
