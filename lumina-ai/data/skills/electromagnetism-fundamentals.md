# Electromagnetism Fundamentals

Electromagnetism governs electricity, magnetism, and light — one of nature's four fundamental forces and the basis of nearly all modern technology. Its great achievement is showing that electricity and magnetism are **two aspects of one phenomenon**, unified by Maxwell, whose equations also revealed that **light is an electromagnetic wave**.

## Electric Charge & Fields

- **Charge** — a fundamental property; positive and negative. Like charges repel, opposite attract (**Coulomb's law**: force ∝ q₁q₂/r²). Charge is conserved and quantized (in units of the electron charge).
- **Electric field (E)** — charges create a field in the space around them; other charges feel a force along it. Field lines point away from positive, toward negative. The field is *how* charges interact at a distance.
- **Voltage (potential)** — potential energy per charge; the "pressure" driving charges. A voltage difference makes charges want to move.

## Circuits: Voltage, Current, Resistance

Charges in motion form **current**:
- **Current (I)** — flow rate of charge (amperes). **Voltage (V)** — the driving potential difference. **Resistance (R)** — opposition to flow.
- **Ohm's law** — V = IR ties them together (see analog-circuit-design-fundamentals for circuits).
- **Power** — P = VI. This is the practical electricity of wires, batteries, and devices.

## Magnetism & Electromagnets

- **Magnetism** — magnets have north/south poles; like poles repel, opposite attract. A **magnetic field (B)** surrounds magnets and moving charges.
- **The key link** — *moving charges (current) create magnetic fields* (Ørsted's discovery). A current-carrying wire is a magnet; coil it (a solenoid) and you get an **electromagnet** — magnetism you can switch on/off and control. This underlies motors, speakers, and MRI machines.
- A magnetic field exerts force on moving charges/currents (the motor principle: F = qv×B).

## Electromagnetic Induction

The reverse link, and one of the most important discoveries in history:
- **Faraday's law** — a *changing* magnetic field induces a voltage (and current) in a nearby conductor. Move a magnet through a coil and you generate electricity.
- This **induction** is how **generators** (mechanical motion → electricity) and **transformers** (changing voltage levels) work — the foundation of the entire electrical grid and power generation.
- Electricity makes magnetism; changing magnetism makes electricity — a beautiful symmetry.

## Maxwell's Unification

James Clerk Maxwell unified it all into **four equations** describing how electric and magnetic fields are generated and interrelate:
- Charges make electric fields; currents and changing electric fields make magnetic fields; changing magnetic fields make electric fields.
- The stunning consequence: a changing E field makes a changing B field makes a changing E field… a self-propagating **electromagnetic wave** traveling at the speed of light — because **light *is* such a wave**. Maxwell unified electricity, magnetism, and optics into one theory.

## Electromagnetic Waves & Light

- **EM waves** — oscillating electric and magnetic fields propagating through space at c (~3×10⁸ m/s), no medium needed.
- **The spectrum** — radio, microwave, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays — all the *same* phenomenon at different frequencies/wavelengths. Visible light is a tiny slice.
- This underlies all wireless communication, optics, and our understanding of light.

Reason about electromagnetism via **charge and electric fields** (Coulomb, voltage), **circuits** (current, resistance, Ohm's law), **magnetism from moving charges** (electromagnets, motors), **induction** (changing magnetic fields make electricity — generators, transformers), **Maxwell's unification** (E and B fields generating each other), and **electromagnetic waves** (light and the whole spectrum). The core insight: electricity and magnetism are one force, and its self-propagating waves are light itself.
