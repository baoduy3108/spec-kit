# Photosynthesis & Plant Biology (Advanced Biology)

Photosynthesis — how plants convert light into chemical energy — is the foundation of nearly all life's energy and a core advanced/gifted-exam (chuyên) biology topic. It's the reverse of respiration, producing the oxygen we breathe and the food chains' base (see ecology-and-ecosystems).

## The Overall Process

- **Photosynthesis**: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ (glucose) + 6O₂.
- Plants capture **light energy** and store it as **chemical energy** in glucose. Occurs in **chloroplasts**, using the green pigment **chlorophyll** (which absorbs red/blue light, reflects green).
- Two stages: light-dependent reactions (capture energy) and light-independent reactions (fix carbon).

## Light-Dependent Reactions

- Occur in the **thylakoid membranes** of chloroplasts.
- **Chlorophyll absorbs light**, exciting electrons; water is **split** (photolysis), releasing **O₂** (the oxygen we breathe) and providing electrons/protons.
- Energy is captured as **ATP** and **NADPH** (energy carriers), via an electron transport chain (like respiration's, in reverse purpose).
- **Requires light**; produces the energy currency for the next stage.

## Light-Independent Reactions (Calvin Cycle)

- Occur in the **stroma** of chloroplasts; don't directly need light (but use the ATP/NADPH from the light reactions).
- **Carbon fixation** — CO₂ is incorporated into organic molecules (via the enzyme RuBisCO) and, using ATP and NADPH, built into **glucose** through the **Calvin cycle**.
- This is where atmospheric carbon becomes sugar — the actual "food-making."

## Chloroplasts & Chlorophyll

- **Chloroplasts** — the organelle of photosynthesis, with stacked thylakoids (grana) and stroma.
- **Chlorophyll and accessory pigments** absorb specific wavelengths; the absorption spectrum matches the action spectrum of photosynthesis.
- **Limiting factors** — light intensity, CO₂ concentration, and temperature limit the rate (a common experimental topic).

## Plant Structure & Transport

- **Roots** absorb water and minerals; **leaves** photosynthesize; **stems** support and transport.
- **Xylem** — transports water and minerals **up** from roots (driven by transpiration — evaporation from leaves pulling water up).
- **Phloem** — transports sugars (products of photosynthesis) **throughout** the plant (translocation, both directions).
- **Stomata** — pores (mostly on leaves) for gas exchange (CO₂ in, O₂/water out), opened/closed by guard cells, balancing photosynthesis against water loss.

## Plant Responses & Relationship to Respiration

- **Tropisms** — growth responses to stimuli (phototropism toward light, gravitropism, via hormones like auxin).
- **Photosynthesis vs respiration** — plants *also* respire (breaking glucose for energy, using O₂, releasing CO₂). Photosynthesis (day, energy storage) and respiration (constant) are complementary opposites; net gas exchange depends on light.

Master plant biology via **photosynthesis overall** (light + CO₂ + water → glucose + O₂ in chloroplasts), the **light-dependent reactions** (splitting water, releasing O₂, making ATP/NADPH), the **Calvin cycle** (fixing CO₂ into glucose using that energy), **chloroplasts and chlorophyll** (with limiting factors), **plant transport** (xylem up via transpiration, phloem for sugars, stomata for gas exchange), and **plant responses**. Understanding how plants turn light into the chemical energy underpinning all food chains is the depth gifted-exam plant biology rewards.
