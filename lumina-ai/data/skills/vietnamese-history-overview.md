# Vietnamese History Overview (Lịch sử Việt Nam)

Vietnamese history is a long story of **state-building, resistance against foreign domination, and national identity** — a recurring theme of struggle for independence. This overview maps the major eras, essential for the Lịch sử exam and for understanding Vietnam.

## Ancient Origins

- **Văn Lang** (Hùng Kings) and **Âu Lạc** (An Dương Vương) — legendary early states in the Red River delta (~7th c. BCE–3rd c. BCE), with the Đông Sơn bronze-drum culture. The roots of national identity.

## Chinese Domination (Bắc thuộc, ~111 BCE–938 CE)

- Over a **thousand years** of Chinese rule, punctuated by uprisings preserving Vietnamese identity:
- **Hai Bà Trưng** (Trưng Sisters, 40 CE) and **Bà Triệu** — early revolts against Chinese rule, iconic symbols of resistance.
- **Ngô Quyền's victory at Bạch Đằng (938)** — ended Chinese domination, winning independence (using stakes in the river to defeat the fleet).

## Independent Dynasties (938–1858)

Centuries of independent monarchies building the nation:
- **Đinh, Early Lê, Lý, Trần, Later Lê, Nguyễn** dynasties. The **Lý** and **Trần** consolidated the state, culture, and Buddhism.
- **Resistance triumphs** — the **Trần dynasty defeated the Mongols** three times (13th c., Trần Hưng Đạo, another Bạch Đằng victory); **Lê Lợi & Nguyễn Trãi** expelled the Ming (15th c.); **Quang Trung (Nguyễn Huệ)** crushed the Qing invasion (1789).
- **Southward expansion (Nam tiến)** — Vietnam extended toward the Mekong delta. The **Nguyễn dynasty** (from 1802) unified the country.

## French Colonization (1858–1945)

- France gradually **colonized** Vietnam (from 1858), making it part of **French Indochina**. Colonial exploitation, but also modernization and new ideas.
- **Resistance movements** — from scholar-led revolts to early nationalist and communist movements. **Hồ Chí Minh** founded the Indochinese Communist Party (1930).

## Struggle for Independence & War (1945–1975)

- **1945** — Hồ Chí Minh declared independence (Democratic Republic of Vietnam) after the August Revolution.
- **First Indochina War** — against France, ending with the decisive **Điện Biên Phủ** victory (1954), which split Vietnam at the 17th parallel (Geneva Accords).
- **Vietnam War (1955–1975)** — North vs South and US involvement; ended with **reunification** (1975) under the North.

## Modern Vietnam

- **Reunification (1976)** as the Socialist Republic of Vietnam.
- **Đổi Mới (1986)** — economic reforms opening to a market economy, driving rapid growth and integration into the world.
- Vietnam today — a fast-developing nation with a strong sense of its long, hard-won independence.

Grasp Vietnamese history as a through-line of **founding (Văn Lang/Âu Lạc)** → **millennium of Chinese domination and repeated revolts (Trưng Sisters, Bạch Đằng 938)** → **independent dynasties defeating Mongols, Ming, and Qing** while expanding south → **French colonization** → the **struggle for independence** (Hồ Chí Minh, Điện Biên Phủ, reunification 1975) → **modern Đổi Mới Vietnam**. The recurring theme — resilience and resistance in defense of independence — is the heart of Lịch sử Việt Nam.
