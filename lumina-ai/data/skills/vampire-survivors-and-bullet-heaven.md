# Vampire Survivors & Bullet-Heaven

The "bullet-heaven" or "survivors-like" genre (Vampire Survivors and its many imitators) inverts bullet hell: instead of dodging enemy bullets, **you auto-fire a growing storm of your own attacks** at overwhelming hordes of enemies. It's a minimal-input power fantasy — you mostly just *move* — that becomes a mesmerizing screen-filling spectacle of destruction. Simple to play, deeply compelling to build.

## Auto-Attack Against Hordes

The core inversion:
- **Weapons fire automatically** — the player usually only controls **movement** (positioning to dodge enemies and aim area attacks). Very low input, high spectacle.
- **Massive enemy hordes** — hundreds/thousands of enemies swarm from all sides. The threat is being *surrounded and overwhelmed* (contact damage), so movement and crowd-management are the survival skill.
- The fantasy: starting weak, ending as an unstoppable death-machine clearing the screen effortlessly. Watching your power *become* overwhelming is the whole appeal.

## Weapon & Passive Leveling + Evolutions

Progression *within a run* is the heart:
- On **leveling up** (from XP gems dropped by kills), you pick **1 of a few random** weapons or passive upgrades — a drafting choice (see roguelike-design's choice-under-uncertainty).
- Weapons **level up** to grow stronger; **passives** boost stats (damage, area, cooldown, projectiles).
- **Evolutions** — a maxed weapon + a specific passive **combines** into a dramatically more powerful evolved form. Discovering and assembling evolutions is the build puzzle and a huge power spike.

## Build Synergies

The strategic depth (from simple parts):
- **Combining weapons + passives** into synergistic **builds** — the emergent power fantasy. Certain combos snowball into absurd screen-clearing power.
- **Drafting under randomness** — you build with what the level-ups offer, adapting toward a coherent build (and required evolution components). This roguelite build-crafting is what drives replayability across runs.
- Many viable builds keep runs varied and experimentation rewarding.

## Escalating Swarms & the Power Curve

- **Escalation** — enemies grow in number, toughness, and variety over the run (often ~15–30 minute runs with a timer), racing your power growth. The tension is *your* scaling vs *their* scaling.
- **The power-fantasy curve** — early game is fragile and tense (few weapons, dangerous); late game is a euphoric screen of carnage as your build peaks. This vulnerable→godlike arc is deliberately engineered and deeply satisfying.
- **Bosses / elite waves** and a survival timer structure the escalation.

## Roguelite Structure

- **Run-based** with permadeath — each run is a fresh build attempt; death ends it.
- **Meta-progression** — permanent unlocks (new characters, weapons, stat upgrades bought with run currency) that add variety and gently ease difficulty over many runs (see roguelike meta-progression).
- **Fast restart** and the "just one more run, one more build" loop.

## Feel & Spectacle

- **Juice and spectacle** — the escalating screen-filling chaos of numbers, effects, and destruction is the payoff (see game-feel-and-juice). Readability still matters (know where *you* are amid the carnage).
- **Minimal-input, maximal-payoff** — the genre's magic ratio: barely touch the controls, watch an epic build unfold.

Design survivors-likes around **auto-attacking against overwhelming hordes** with only movement input, **level-up drafting of weapons/passives and evolutions** that build into **synergistic runaway builds**, an **escalating swarm** racing a deliberate **vulnerable-to-godlike power curve**, wrapped in **roguelite runs with meta-progression** — delivering a low-effort, high-spectacle power fantasy where assembling the perfect build is the addictive core.
