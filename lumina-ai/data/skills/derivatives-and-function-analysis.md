# Derivatives & Function Analysis (THPT Calculus)

The derivative measures **instantaneous rate of change / slope** (see calculus-intuition); here we develop the *rules* for computing it and its main THPT application: **analyzing and graphing functions** (khảo sát hàm số) and solving optimization problems. A major exam topic.

## Differentiation Rules

Computing derivatives systematically:
- **Power rule** — d/dx(xⁿ) = n·xⁿ⁻¹. **Constant** → 0; **sum/difference** differentiate term by term.
- **Product rule** — (fg)' = f'g + fg'.
- **Quotient rule** — (f/g)' = (f'g − fg')/g².
- **Chain rule** — for composite functions, (f(g(x)))' = f'(g(x))·g'(x). The most important and error-prone.
- **Standard derivatives** — of trig (sin'=cos, cos'=−sin), exponential (eˣ'=eˣ), and logarithmic (ln x)'=1/x functions.

## Monotonicity & Extrema

The derivative reveals where a function rises and falls:
- **f'(x) > 0** → f is **increasing**; **f'(x) < 0** → **decreasing**. Sign of f' gives monotonic intervals.
- **Critical points** — where f'(x) = 0 (or undefined) — candidates for **maxima/minima**.
- **First-derivative test** — f' changing + to − → local max; − to + → local min.
- **Second-derivative test** — f''(x) < 0 at a critical point → max; f''(x) > 0 → min.

## Concavity & Inflection

- **f''(x) > 0** → **concave up** (∪); **f''(x) < 0** → **concave down** (∩).
- **Inflection points** — where concavity changes (f'' = 0 and switches sign). Shapes the curve's bending.

## Curve Sketching (Khảo sát hàm số)

The classic THPT procedure — fully analyze and graph a function:
1. **Domain**.
2. **Derivative f'** → increasing/decreasing intervals and **extrema**.
3. **Limits/asymptotes** — behavior at infinity and near undefined points (vertical/horizontal/oblique asymptotes — see limits-and-continuity).
4. **(Second derivative)** → concavity and inflection.
5. **Table of variation** and **sketch** the graph.
This systematic analysis is a staple exam question.

## Optimization & Related Rates

Applying derivatives to real problems:
- **Optimization** — to maximize/minimize a quantity, express it as a function, find critical points (f'=0), and identify the extreme (checking it's a max/min and within constraints). E.g. largest area for given perimeter, minimal cost.
- **Related rates** — when quantities change together, differentiate a relating equation with respect to time to link their rates (e.g. how fast a shadow lengthens).
- **Tangent lines** — y − f(a) = f'(a)(x − a), using the derivative as slope.

Master derivatives and function analysis via the **differentiation rules** (power, product, quotient, and especially the chain rule; standard function derivatives), using **f' for monotonicity and extrema** (sign of f', critical points, first/second-derivative tests), **f'' for concavity and inflection**, the full **curve-sketching procedure** (domain → f' → asymptotes → f'' → graph), and **optimization/related rates** applications. Turning the derivative into a tool for understanding a function's entire behavior — and finding its extremes — is the core of THPT calculus.
