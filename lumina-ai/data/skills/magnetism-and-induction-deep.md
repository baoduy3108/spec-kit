# Magnetism & Electromagnetic Induction (Advanced Physics)

Magnetism and induction form the quantitative heart of advanced/gifted-exam (chuyên) electromagnetism (từ trường và cảm ứng). They connect moving charges to magnetic fields and, via induction, to the generation of electricity — the principle behind generators and the entire electrical grid.

## Magnetic Force on Charges & Currents

- **Lorentz force** — a charge moving in a magnetic field feels **F = qv×B** = qvB·sinθ, **perpendicular** to both velocity and field. It changes direction, not speed — so charges move in **circles/helices** in a uniform field (used in cyclotrons, mass spectrometers).
- **Force on a current-carrying wire**: **F = BIL·sinθ** — the basis of electric motors.
- **Right-hand rule** — determines the force direction.

## Magnetic Fields from Currents

Moving charges *create* magnetic fields (Ørsted, see electromagnetism-fundamentals):
- **Straight wire** — B = μ₀I/(2πr), circles around the wire.
- **Solenoid/coil** — B = μ₀nI inside, a uniform field like a bar magnet (an electromagnet).
- **Ampère's law** — relates the field around a loop to the enclosed current (a Gauss's-law analog for magnetism); gives fields for symmetric current distributions.

## Faraday's & Lenz's Laws (Induction)

The central phenomenon:
- **Faraday's law** — a **changing magnetic flux** induces an EMF: **EMF = −dΦ/dt**, where flux Φ = B·A·cosθ. Change B, the area, or the angle → induce a voltage.
- **Lenz's law** (the minus sign) — the induced current **opposes** the change causing it (conservation of energy). A magnet pushed into a coil induces a current that repels it.
- This is how *motion or changing fields generate electricity* — the inverse of the motor effect.

## Motional EMF

- A conductor of length L moving at speed v through a field B develops **EMF = BLv** (the charges feel the Lorentz force, separating). A rod sliding on rails is the classic setup — a simple generator, and a common exam problem.

## Inductance

- **Self-inductance (L)** — a changing current in a coil induces a back-EMF opposing the change: EMF = −L·dI/dt. Inductors resist current *changes* (like inertia for current).
- **Energy stored** in an inductor's magnetic field: W = ½LI².
- **Mutual inductance** — a changing current in one coil induces EMF in a nearby one (the transformer principle).

## Generators & Transformers

- **Generator** — rotating a coil in a magnetic field continuously changes flux, inducing an alternating EMF (AC) — mechanical energy → electrical (see ac-circuits-and-impedance). The reverse of a motor.
- **Transformer** — two coils sharing a core; a changing current in the primary induces voltage in the secondary, scaled by the turns ratio (V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p). Steps AC voltage up/down — vital for power transmission. Works only with AC (needs *changing* flux).

Master magnetism and induction via the **Lorentz force** (F = qv×B, circular motion; F = BIL on wires — the motor effect), **fields from currents** (wire, solenoid, Ampère's law), **Faraday's and Lenz's laws** (changing flux induces an opposing EMF — the generator effect), **motional EMF** (BLv), **inductance** (back-EMF, energy ½LI²), and **generators/transformers** (converting motion↔electricity, scaling AC voltage). The deep symmetry — currents make magnetism, changing magnetism makes currents — is the core of gifted-exam electromagnetism.
