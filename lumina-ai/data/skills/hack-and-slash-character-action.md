# Hack-and-Slash / Character-Action

Character-action games (Devil May Cry, Bayonetta, Ninja Gaiden) are the high-skill-ceiling cousin of action combat: fast, stylish, combo-driven melee where mastery lets players string together dazzling, creative attack sequences. Where souls-likes are deliberate, character-action is **fast, expressive, and about looking cool while staying in control**.

## Deep Combo Systems

The heart is a **rich, expressive combo system**:
- **Large movesets** — many attacks across weapons/stances/modes, launchers, aerials, that combine in countless ways. The vocabulary is broad on purpose.
- **Cancels** — interrupting one move into another (jump-cancels, weapon-switch cancels) is the key to advanced combos; the *cancel rules* define the ceiling and let skilled players invent sequences the designer never scripted.
- **Player expression** — there's no single "correct" combo; players craft their own style. This creativity is the genre's soul — combat as improvisational performance.

## Style / Ranking Meters

The signature mechanic: a **style meter** (DMC's D→S rank) that rewards **varied, uninterrupted, skillful** play:
- Rewards **variety** (don't repeat the same move), avoiding damage, and flair. Getting hit or spamming tanks your rank.
- This pushes players *toward* mastery — you're incentivized to use your whole toolkit stylishly, not spam the optimal move. The meter turns "win the fight" into "win it *beautifully*," which is the entire appeal.
- Often ties to rewards (currency, feedback) reinforcing the loop.

## Enemy & Encounter Design

Enemies are designed as **combo fodder and rhythm**:
- Enemies that can be launched, juggled, and comboed — the combat sandbox needs willing "toys."
- **Variety and mixed groups** create the challenge of maintaining style while managing threats.
- **Bosses** as spectacle set-pieces testing mastery.
- Balance between letting players go wild and enemies that punish carelessness (armor, aggression) so combos are *earned*, not free.

## Advanced Mechanics

- **Dodge-offset / dodge-cancel** (Bayonetta) — dodge without losing your combo progress, rewarding timing.
- **Just-frame / precise timing inputs** — frame-perfect versions of moves with extra power, a mastery layer.
- **Witch-time / focus** — reward for perfect dodges (slow-mo openings).
- **Weapon/style switching mid-combo** for expression and adaptation.

These reward practice with expanded expressive range — the "practice makes stylish" loop.

## Skill Expression & Depth

- **Easy to play, extremely hard to master** — mashing works at the surface; the depth is bottomless for those who dig. This wide skill gap is intentional and celebrated (combo videos, superplays).
- **Feel** — fast, fluid, responsive, flashy; hitstop and effects sell impact without slowing the flow.
- **Player fantasy** — being an impossibly cool, in-control action hero. Every design serves letting players *feel* and *look* masterful.

Design character-action around a **deep, cancel-rich combo system** that enables player-authored expression, a **style meter** that incentivizes varied skillful play, **enemies built as a combo sandbox**, and **advanced timing mechanics** that reward mastery — all fast, fluid, and flashy, with a bottomless skill ceiling that turns combat into stylish, improvisational performance.
