# Gravitation & Kepler's Laws (Advanced Physics)

Gravitation governs falling apples and orbiting planets alike — Newton's insight that the *same* force explains both. This advanced/gifted-exam (chuyên) topic quantifies gravity, orbits, and satellites, and connects to Kepler's empirical laws of planetary motion.

## Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation

- Every mass attracts every other: **F = G·m₁m₂/r²** (G ≈ 6.67×10⁻¹¹), along the line joining them.
- An **inverse-square law** (like Coulomb's — see electrostatics). It's always attractive, extremely weak per unit mass but dominant at astronomical scales (where masses are enormous).
- **Weight** = gravitational force near a planet: near Earth's surface, g = GM/R² ≈ 9.8 m/s².

## Gravitational Field & Potential Energy

- **Gravitational field g** — force per unit mass (N/kg); g = GM/r² around a mass M, pointing inward.
- **Gravitational potential energy** — U = −GMm/r (negative, zero at infinity; more negative closer in). Work must be done to move away.
- **Near the surface**, U ≈ mgh (the familiar approximation for small heights — see newtonian-mechanics).

## Orbital Motion

- A **satellite in circular orbit** — gravity provides the **centripetal force**: GMm/r² = mv²/r, giving **orbital speed v = √(GM/r)** (faster in lower orbits).
- **Orbital period** T = 2π√(r³/GM) (Kepler's third law form).
- **Geostationary orbit** — period = 24 h, so the satellite stays above one point (communications).

## Escape Velocity

- **Escape velocity** — the minimum speed to leave a body's gravity forever (KE = |U|): **v_esc = √(2GM/R)** (≈11.2 km/s for Earth). Independent of the escaping object's mass.
- Below it → bound orbit; above → escape.

## Kepler's Laws

Empirical laws of planetary motion, later explained by Newton's gravity:
1. **Law of orbits** — planets move in **ellipses** with the Sun at one focus (not perfect circles).
2. **Law of areas** — a planet sweeps **equal areas in equal times** — so it moves *faster* when closer to the Sun (a consequence of angular-momentum conservation — see rigid-body-and-rotational-dynamics).
3. **Law of periods** — **T² ∝ r³** (the square of the period is proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis). Relates orbital size to period across all planets.

## Weightlessness

- **Free fall / weightlessness** — astronauts in orbit *feel* weightless not because gravity is absent (it's ~90% of surface gravity) but because they're in continuous **free fall** around Earth — falling at the same rate as their spacecraft. "Zero-g" is really perpetual falling.

Master gravitation via **Newton's universal law** (F = Gm₁m₂/r², inverse-square, weight and g), the **gravitational field and potential energy** (g = GM/r², U = −GMm/r), **orbital motion** (gravity as centripetal force, v = √(GM/r), periods), **escape velocity** (√(2GM/R)), **Kepler's three laws** (elliptical orbits, equal areas/angular momentum, T² ∝ r³), and **weightlessness as free fall**. The unifying triumph — one inverse-square law explains both falling objects and planetary orbits — is central to gifted-exam gravitation.
