# Special Relativity (Advanced Physics)

Einstein's special relativity (1905) overturned intuitions about space and time, showing they're relative to the observer's motion, unified into **spacetime**. It's a modern-physics topic in advanced/gifted-exam contexts — conceptually startling but built on two simple postulates.

## The Two Postulates

1. **Principle of relativity** — the laws of physics are the same in all **inertial** (non-accelerating) reference frames. No experiment can detect "absolute" uniform motion.
2. **Constancy of light speed** — light travels at **c** (~3×10⁸ m/s) in vacuum for *all* observers, regardless of the source's or observer's motion.

The second postulate is the shock: if everyone measures light at the same speed, then space and time themselves must adjust. Everything else follows.

## Time Dilation

- **Moving clocks run slow** — a clock moving relative to you ticks slower by the **Lorentz factor** γ = 1/√(1 − v²/c²): **Δt = γ·Δt₀**.
- The faster you move (closer to c), the more time dilates (γ → ∞ as v → c). Confirmed by fast-moving particles (muons) and atomic clocks.
- **Proper time** (Δt₀) is measured in the frame where the events happen at the same place.

## Length Contraction

- **Moving objects contract** along the direction of motion: **L = L₀/γ** (shorter than the rest length L₀).
- Space and time are complementary — what one observer sees as time dilation, the geometry pairs with length contraction.

## Relativity of Simultaneity

- **Simultaneity is relative** — two events simultaneous in one frame are *not* simultaneous in another moving frame. There's no universal "now." This is the deepest conceptual break, resolving apparent paradoxes (like the twin/ladder paradoxes).

## The Lorentz Transformation

- The equations converting coordinates (x, t) between frames moving at relative velocity v — replacing the classical Galilean transformation. They mix space and time (t' depends on both x and t), encoding dilation, contraction, and simultaneity.
- At low speeds (v ≪ c), γ ≈ 1 and they reduce to everyday Galilean physics — relativity contains Newtonian mechanics as a low-speed limit.
- **Velocity addition** is relativistic (velocities don't simply add; nothing exceeds c).

## Relativistic Momentum & Energy

- **Momentum** p = γmv (grows without bound as v → c, so infinite force can't reach c).
- **Energy** — **E = γmc²**; at rest, the famous **E₀ = mc²** (rest energy — mass *is* concentrated energy, see nuclear-and-particle-physics). Total energy = rest energy + kinetic.
- **E² = (pc)² + (mc²)²** — the energy-momentum relation; for massless photons (m=0), E = pc.

## The Speed-of-Light Limit

- Nothing with mass can reach c (it would need infinite energy). c is the universe's ultimate speed limit and the conversion factor between space and time, and mass and energy.

Master special relativity via the **two postulates** (relativity of physics; constant light speed), **time dilation** (moving clocks slow, γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²)), **length contraction** (moving objects shorten), **relativity of simultaneity** (no universal now), the **Lorentz transformation** (mixing space and time, reducing to Newton at low speed), and **relativistic momentum/energy** (E = γmc², rest energy E₀ = mc², the c speed limit). The startling lesson: space and time are relative and unified, all forced by light's constant speed.
