# Vietnamese Geography (Địa lý Việt Nam)

Vietnam's geography — its S-shaped territory, tropical climate, long coastline, and deltas — shapes its agriculture, population, and development. This overview covers the physical land, people, and economy, essential for the Địa lý exam.

## Physical Geography

- **Location & shape** — a long, narrow S-shaped country in Southeast Asia along the eastern Indochina Peninsula, bordering China, Laos, and Cambodia, with a ~3,260 km **coastline** on the East Sea (South China Sea).
- **Terrain** — **¾ hills and mountains** (Trường Sơn range along the west, the northern highlands with Fansipan, the highest peak), and **¼ lowland deltas**. Two great **deltas**: the **Red River Delta** (north) and the **Mekong Delta** (south) — flat, fertile, densely populated rice bowls.
- **Climate** — **tropical monsoon**, hot and humid, with distinct wet and dry seasons; the north has a cooler winter, the south is tropical year-round. Prone to typhoons, floods, and drought.
- **Rivers** — the **Red River** and **Mekong** are lifelines for water, transport, and agriculture. Dense river/canal networks.

## Natural Resources

- **Agricultural land** — fertile deltas for rice (a top global exporter), plus coffee, rubber, and other crops in the highlands.
- **Minerals** — coal (north), bauxite, and **offshore oil and gas**.
- **Marine resources** — a rich fishing zone and long coast (fisheries, aquaculture, tourism).
- **Forests and biodiversity** — though deforestation and conservation are concerns.

## Regions

- **Northern** (Red River Delta, northern mountains) — historic heartland, Hà Nội the capital.
- **Central** (narrow coastal strip, Trường Sơn, Central Highlands) — Đà Nẵng, Huế; coffee and coastal economy.
- **Southern** (Mekong Delta, southeast) — Hồ Chí Minh City (the economic hub), the agricultural Mekong Delta.

## Population & Urbanization

- **~100 million people** — one of the most populous countries; **54 ethnic groups** (the Kinh majority plus diverse minorities, especially in highlands).
- **Dense in the deltas and cities**, sparse in the mountains. **Rapid urbanization** and a young, growing (now aging-transition) population — a demographic driver of the economy.

## Economy

- Transformed by **Đổi Mới** reforms (see vietnamese-history) into a fast-growing market economy:
- **Agriculture** — rice, coffee, seafood, cashew (major exports), though a declining share of GDP.
- **Industry & manufacturing** — the growth engine: textiles, electronics, and export manufacturing (a rising player in global supply chains).
- **Services** — tourism (spectacular coast, heritage), finance, and trade.
- Deep integration into the world economy (exports, FDI, trade agreements).

Understand Vietnamese geography via its **physical land** (S-shape, ¾ mountains + fertile Red River and Mekong deltas, tropical monsoon climate, long coastline, great rivers), **natural resources** (rice-growing deltas, minerals, oil/gas, marine wealth), **three regions** (north/central/south), **population** (~100M, 54 ethnicities, delta-concentrated, rapidly urbanizing), and the **Đổi Mới economy** (from agriculture toward export manufacturing and services). How the land and climate shape agriculture, settlement, and development is the geographic core of Địa lý Việt Nam.
